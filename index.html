Tankō

Support

Tankō reads your own manga, comics, e-books and PDFs and plays your audiobooks, all in one library.

Common questions

How do I add books? Tap + in the library and choose Add Files, or open a file in Tankō from the Files app, AirDrop or another app. Tankō reads CBZ, CBR, PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, M4B and MP3, and whole folders. Books bought in other stores are usually copy-protected (DRM) and can't be opened.

Can I use my own server? Yes. Tap + › From a Catalogue and add your Komga, Kavita or Calibre-Web OPDS address, or an Audiobookshelf server. Komga, Kavita and Audiobookshelf keep your place in step with the server.

My place doesn't sync to my iPad. Both devices need the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, and Settings › Library & iCloud › iCloud sync on in Tankō. The book files themselves stay on each device; add the same file on both.

Read aloud sounds robotic. Download a natural voice in Settings › Read Aloud, or install a Premium or Enhanced voice in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices.

Restoring Tankō Pro. Tankō Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Open Settings in Tankō, tap Tankō Pro, then Restore Purchase.
