Tankō

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

In short: Tankō collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no server and receives nothing from the app.

Your files

The books and audiobooks you add stay on your device, in Tankō's own storage. Tankō never uploads them.

iCloud

If iCloud is on for Tankō, your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, clips, collections, reading statistics and saved words sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database. Apple stores this data; the developer cannot see it. Turn it off in Settings › Library & iCloud or in iOS Settings.

Network requests Tankō makes

  • Cover lookup: when a book has no cover, Tankō can send its title and author to Apple's iTunes Search API and to Open Library (Internet Archive) to find one. Nothing else is sent. Turn it off in Settings › Library & iCloud. Apple's and Open Library's own privacy policies apply to those requests.
  • Natural voices: only if you tap Download in Settings › Read Aloud, Tankō downloads voice files from Hugging Face (about 230 MB for English, about 200 MB for 31 other languages). Nothing about you or your books is sent. The voices then run on your device.
  • Your catalogues and servers: if you add an OPDS catalogue (for example your own Komga, Kavita or Calibre-Web server) or an Audiobookshelf server, Tankō connects only to the address you entered, to list and download books and, for Komga, Kavita and Audiobookshelf, to keep your reading or listening position in step with that server. A password you give stays in this device's Keychain and is sent only to that server.

On your device

Translation, text recognition, read aloud, recaps (written by Apple Intelligence from pages you've already read) and statistics run on your device using Apple's frameworks. The Apple Watch app talks only to your own iPhone.

Purchases

Tankō Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Tankō only learns that Pro is unlocked and never sees your payment details.

Children

Tankō does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
